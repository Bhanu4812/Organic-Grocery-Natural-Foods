Featured farm story

Three generations at Willow Creek.

At Willow Creek, farming wisdom travels between generations. Cover crops rest the soil, pollinator borders support biodiversity and each field is harvested in smaller, more precise rounds.

That extra care gives greens more time to develop flavour - and keeps the farm adaptable through changing seasons.
